About PakZameen

PakZameen (پاک زمین — "Pure Land") is Pakistan's smart property portal — a single place to buy, sell and rent houses, flats, plots and commercial property across the country, with verified agencies, transparent pricing tools and practical market data.

Our mission

Property is the biggest purchase most Pakistanis will ever make — yet the market runs on word of mouth, scattered listings and unverifiable claims. We are building the portal we wished existed: every listing searchable, every agency verified, every area's price trend visible, and every buyer armed with the precautions that prevent fraud.

How PakZameen works

For buyers & renters: search by city, area, type and budget; compare properties side by side; check area price trends and rental yields; and contact advertisers directly. Our safety checklist walks you through verifying documents before you pay a single rupee.

For agencies: register free, post listings, boost the ones that matter, and earn a share of the advertising revenue your pages generate. Every agency passes CNIC and office verification before the badge appears.

Tools: mortgage calculator, plot area converter, construction cost estimator and market comparison tables — free for everyone.

A note on our data

PakZameen is currently in its concept launch phase: statistics and sample listings shown on the portal are illustrative while our agency network onboards. We mark sample data clearly wherever it appears, and we never present estimates as verified market figures. Always confirm prices against recent registry (FBR/DC) rates and get independent legal advice before transacting.
